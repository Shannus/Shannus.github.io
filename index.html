01 · About

From data pipelines to platform engineering

I spent the first stretch of my career building data infrastructure: streaming pipelines on Kafka, batch processing with PySpark, and the Kubernetes clusters that ran them. That work taught me to think in flows, where data comes from, what could break it in transit, and how you'd actually know if it did.

I now bring that thinking to the Cloud Platform Engineering team at Tradu (FXCM / Stratos Group), where I help build and operate the AWS EKS platform behind the firm's trading systems, spanning Kubernetes networking, GitOps delivery, identity, and observability. The stakes are different when infrastructure sits under a live trading platform instead of a dashboard, and that's exactly the kind of pressure I wanted to build under.
